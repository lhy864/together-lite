# Third-party components

Together Lite includes Electron (MIT), Chromium and its third-party components (see the bundled Electron/Chromium license files), Vue (MIT), Fastify and its plugins (MIT), ws (MIT), and qrcode (MIT).

The HTTPS companion program is Caddy, licensed under Apache License 2.0. Its license is distributed as resources/tools/CADDY-LICENSE. Source and official builds: https://github.com/caddyserver/caddy .

The included sample MP4 is a locally generated FFmpeg test pattern with a synthetic test tone, created for playback verification. No third-party film is included.
